Factory City art assets

Derived from asset packs by Kenney (https://kenney.nl), all licensed
Creative Commons Zero (CC0) - free for personal and commercial use,
no attribution required (but appreciated!).
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The kits ship as 3D models; each chosen model was rendered to a 512x512
isometric PNG (yaw 45 deg, pitch 30 deg, orthographic) by
tools/kenney-city-render.html and cropped with tools/autocrop-png.js.

Sources:
- Factory Kit (https://kenney.nl/assets/factory-kit)
  the central factory "HQ" yard (machine, hopper, crane, robot arm),
  concrete ground tile, cog decoration, active conveyor pieces, cargo boxes,
  crane magnet and service stairs.
- City Kit (Commercial) (https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial)
  the office towers (small building -> glass skyscraper) and the shops.
- City Kit (Industrial) (https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-industrial)
  the factory, power-plant and warehouse buildings, plus standalone chimneys
  and tanks that appear as the production district expands.
- City Kit (Suburban) (https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-suburban)
  the houses, trees, planter, fence and stone-path tile.
- Coaster Kit (https://kenney.nl/assets/coaster-kit)
  the amusement-park plot (entrance arch, station, steel coaster loop),
  the food stall, bench, round tree, flowers and the grass ground tile.
- Mini Market (https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-market)
  the shopping-cart prop and the citizen character.
- Car Kit (https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit)
  the complete isometric delivery vehicle and the compact yard loader used on
  the outbound service road and factory floor.
